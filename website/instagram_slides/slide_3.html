Webcam posture tools burn laptop battery and risk privacy. FixMySpine connects to Apple's CoreMotion API directly over low-energy Bluetooth.

STAGE 01
Sensor Ingestion
CMHeadphoneMotionManager
Direct 60Hz IMU motion stream. Reads pitch (nod) and roll (tilt) angular velocity over BLE.
Latency < 16ms
STAGE 02
Biometric Filter
sqrt(nod² + 0.49·side²)
Compound vector math with a 10s grace filter. Ignores natural sips of water or brief head turns.
Zero False Positives
STAGE 03

100% offline macOS processing. Zero camera permissions, zero cloud calls, <0.1% CPU.
